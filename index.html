Molehill

A QUIET GARDEN PUZZLE · SUPPORT

Dig around the sleeping moles, mark their naps, and bring every bed into bloom.

How to play

Tap a patch of turf to dig it. Numbers count the moles napping in the patches touching them.

Hold a patch to plant a marker where a mole is asleep. The Dig/Mark button swaps the two if you would rather tap.

Tap a number once all its moles are marked to dig the rest around it.

Drag to move around the garden and pinch to zoom.

Common questions

A bed is fenced off. What now?
You woke a mole there. Bring the beds around it into bloom and the fence comes down, with the bed just as you left it.

I dug a mole and nothing bad happened.
If there was no way to work out a safe patch, the guess is forgiven and the mole is simply marked for you.

What does the robin do?
Call her, then tap a patch you are unsure about. She digs it if it is safe and marks it if a mole is there. She comes back every three beds you bring into bloom.

Is my progress backed up?
Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?
Pause the game and use the Sound, Music and Haptics switches.

How do I replay the tutorial?
Tap Tutorial at the bottom of the Home screen.
